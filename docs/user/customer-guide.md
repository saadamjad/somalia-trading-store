# Customer Guide — Somalia Trading Store

A plain-language walkthrough of shopping and managing your account on the Somalia Trading Store website.

## Creating an account and signing in

Go to **Register** to create an account with your name, email, phone (optional), and a password. Once registered, you can sign in any time from **Login** using your email and password. Your session stays signed in for 30 days unless you sign out.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot Password** — a reset link is generated for your account. (Note: in the current version of the site, this link isn't emailed to you automatically — see your store administrator if you need help resetting your password while email delivery is being set up.)

## Browsing and searching products

- **Shop** lists all products, organized into categories (Construction Materials, Road Interlocks, Fishing Products).
- Click a category to see just its products, or use the filter panel to narrow by attributes like subcategory or availability.
- Use **Search** to find a product by name.
- Each product page shows photos, a description, specifications, price, and current availability.

## Cart and wishlist

- Click **Add to Cart** on any product to add it to your cart. Adjust quantity or remove items from the **Cart** page.
- If you're signed in, your cart is saved to your account and will still be there next time you visit from any device. If you're not signed in, your cart is remembered only on this browser.
- Click the heart/wishlist icon on a product to save it for later. Your wishlist works the same way — saved to your account once you're signed in.
- Before you can check out, the site double-checks that everything in your cart is still in stock at the quantity you want.

## Checking out

1. From your cart, click **Checkout**.
2. Choose a saved address or enter a new shipping address (recipient name, phone, address, city).
3. Add an optional note for the order.
4. Review your order and place it.

**Important: this store does not currently collect online payment.** There is no card entry or payment step at checkout. Your order is created and confirmed, but it is fulfilled and paid for through arrangements made directly with Somalia Trading (e.g. by phone, in person, or however your existing relationship with the business works) — not through the website. Every order starts with a status of **Pending** and a payment status of **Not Paid**, and no order is ever automatically marked as paid.

After you place an order, you'll see an order confirmation with your order number, and the order will appear in your order history.

## Tracking your order

Go to **Account → Orders** to see all your past orders. Click into any order to see:
- Its current status (Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered, or Cancelled)
- A timeline showing when each status change happened
- The items, quantities, and prices you ordered (these are locked in at the time you ordered — even if a product's price changes later, your order still shows what you actually paid)
- Your shipping address for that order

Order status and payment status are tracked separately — an order can be "Confirmed" while still showing "Not Paid," since payment is arranged outside the website (see above).

## Requesting a refund

If something's wrong with an order that's already been confirmed, processing, shipped, or delivered, you can request a refund from that order's detail page (**Account → Orders → [your order]**). Choose a reason (damaged, wrong item, not as described, no longer needed, or other) and add details if you like.

An admin will review your request and either approve or reject it, with a note explaining their decision — you'll see that note and a notification when it's resolved. Note: a refund *request* is a request for the business to agree you should get your money back — since the site doesn't process payments directly, approving a request doesn't automatically transfer money back to you; that happens through the same direct arrangement as your original payment.

You can only have one open refund request per order at a time. Orders that are still Pending can simply be cancelled instead (nothing was paid yet), and Cancelled orders can't have a refund requested against them (they were never fulfilled).

## Requesting a quote

Some products (or bulk/custom orders) work better through a quote than a straightforward purchase. From the **Quote Request** page, you can:
- Add one or more products with the quantity you want
- Optionally suggest a price you'd hope to pay
- Leave contact details and a message describing what you need

You don't need an account to submit a quote request — it's a business inquiry, not a purchase. If you're signed in, though, your quote will show up under **Account → Quotes**, and you'll get an in-app notification when the business responds.

Once an admin reviews your request and sets real pricing, the quote's status becomes "Quoted" and you can **Accept** or **Decline** it. If you accept, the business will follow up to convert it into a real order at the price you agreed to — note that accepting doesn't automatically create the order; an admin finalizes that step. (If you submitted your quote as a guest, you'll need to register or log in before it can be converted into an order.)

## Managing your account

Under **Account**, you can:
- **Profile** — update your name, email, phone, and change your password.
- **Addresses** — save multiple shipping addresses (e.g. home and office) and mark one as your default.
- **Notifications** — see updates about your order status changes, refund request decisions, and quote responses. A badge in the header shows how many are unread.
